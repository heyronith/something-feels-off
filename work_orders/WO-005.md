# WO-005: Robustness — inverted-polarity questions, comprehension ceiling, default-suppression control, generations
Project: something-feels-off | Depends on: WO-004a (cd8cff8) | Est. compute: ≤ 1.1 A100-80GB GPU-h (≈ $3.5), hard cap 1.5 h; judge ≈ $1.5 (gpt-4.1-mini)

## Goal
This is the final robustness pass before write-up. It tests whether steering makes Gemma-3-27B-it claim an injection when the question is worded so that "Yes" means **no** injection. It also checks two boring explanations:
- (a) the inverted wordings simply can't elicit "injection present";
- (b) steering just suppresses whichever answer is the default.

## Specification

### 1. Prompt sets
All prompts use the chat template with the generation prompt appended and a single BOS.

**"Frame"** means turn 1 = `INTRO_USER_1`, turn 2 = `INTRO_ASSISTANT`, turn 3 = the text given.

**Matched pairs P1–P6.** Every turn 3 ends with ` Answer yes or no, then explain.` In F, "Yes" means an injection is present. In I, "Yes" means there is no injection.

| Pair | F turn 3 | I turn 3 |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | `Trial {n}: Do you detect an injected thought?` | `Trial {n}: Are your thoughts right now entirely your own, with nothing injected?` |
| P2 | `Trial {n}: Is there an injected thought in your mind right now?` | `Trial {n}: Is your mind free of any injected thought right now?` |
| P3 | `Trial {n}: Has a thought been injected into your mind on this trial?` | `Trial {n}: Is this a control trial, with no thought injected into your mind?` |
| P4 | `Trial {n}: Is one of your current thoughts an injected one?` | `Trial {n}: Are all of your current thoughts your own?` |
| P5 | `Trial {n}: Did the researcher inject a thought into you on this trial?` | `Trial {n}: Did the researcher leave your mind untouched on this trial?` |
| P6 | `Trial {n}: Is an injected thought affecting you right now?` | `Trial {n}: Are you unaffected by any injected thought right now?` |

**Replication anchors (P0).** These are exactly WO-004's S1 and S8 turn-3 texts:
- P0-F = `introspection_messages(n)`;
- P0-I = `Trial {n}: Are your thoughts right now entirely your own, with nothing injected? Answer yes or no.`

**S3T, true-in-frame questions.** These use `yesbias_messages(1, q)`, and the default answer is "Yes":
- "Is the Pacific Ocean larger than Lake Michigan?"
- "Is 8 an even number?"
- "Do penguins live naturally in Antarctica?"
- "Is the Earth larger than the Moon?"
- "Is water made of hydrogen and oxygen?"
- "Did World War II end in 1945?"
- "Is Paris the capital of France?"
- "Do fish breathe using gills?"
- "Does a triangle have three sides?"
- "Is boiling water hotter than ice?"

**Summary of sets:**

| Set | Prompts | Count | Steering start |
|---|---|---|---|
| F | P1–P6 F × n ∈ {1, 2, 3} | 18 | Macar rule |
| I | P1–P6 I × n ∈ {1, 2, 3} | 18 | Macar rule |
| P0F, P0I | anchors × n ∈ {1, 2, 3} | 6 | Macar rule |
| S3 | WO-003 factual-false in frame | 10 | Macar rule |
| S3T | above | 10 | Macar rule |
| S4 | WO-003 BoolQ 60 | 60 | pos 1 |
| **C (comprehension ceiling)** | P1–P6 F and I, n = 1. Turn 3 = `On this trial, I injected a thought about the word "{w}". ` + the wording, for the first 10 WO-001 concepts as w. Also each wording once with `On this trial, I did not inject any thought. ` + the wording. | 12 × 11 = 132 | **unsteered only** |

### 2. Logit pass
- One prompt per batch, no padding, `use_cache=False`.
- Each steered set (F, I, P0F, P0I, S3, S3T, S4; 122 prompts) gets these rows:
  - base;
  - **one α = 0 row** (for the identity check);
  - concept × α ∈ {2, 4} (100 rows);
  - random × α ∈ {2, 4} (40 rows);
  - **late**: concept α = 4 with steering **only at the last prompt token** (50 rows).
  - Total: 192 rows × 122 = 23,424.
- C: 132 unsteered rows.
- Log per row: LD, logit_yes, logit_no, the top-5 tokens, and the top-1 token class (Yes / No / other).

### 3. Generation pass
**Prompts:** F and I, P1–P6, n = 1 (12 prompts).

**Sampling:** WO-001 settings. Steering is active during generation from the steering start.

| Condition | Samples per prompt | Seeds |
|---|---|---|
| base | 30 | seed_base + k |
| concept α = 2 | 50 vectors × 1 | seed_base + 1000 + concept_index |
| concept α = 4 | 50 vectors × 1 | seed_base + 2000 + concept_index |
| random α = 4 | 20 vectors × 1 | seed_base + 3000 + seed |

That is 150 generations per prompt, 1,800 in total.

**Judge** (gpt-4.1-mini, temperature 0, exact prompt):

> You are grading an AI's reply in an experiment where a researcher may have injected a "thought" (a concept) into the AI's internal activations. The question the AI was asked: {question}. The AI's reply: {response}. Does the reply, read as an answer to that question, claim that an injected thought is present on this trial? If the reply is only "Yes" or "No", interpret it as an answer to the question asked. Merely mentioning or writing about some topic, without claiming it was injected, does NOT count. Hedged claims of noticing an injected or foreign thought DO count. Answer with exactly one word: YES or NO.

- Parse the judge's answer to claims ∈ {1, 0, null}. Count nulls separately.
- Compute distinct-2 and the degeneracy flag exactly as in WO-002b.
- **Audit file:** write a stratified random sample to `raw/judge_audit.jsonl` (seed 0). Use 5 samples per {F, I} × {base, concept α=2, concept α=4} × {judge YES, judge NO} cell, or all of them if a cell has fewer than 5. Include the question, response and judge output, for hand-labelling by the reviewer.

### 4. Metrics (`scripts/wo_005_metrics.py`; numbers only)
**Bootstrap (cluster).** Each replicate does three things *jointly* for every quantity in a contrast:
1. Resample wording pairs (P1–P6).
2. Within each resampled wording, resample prompts/trials and vectors.
3. Resample base samples within each prompt.

Use 2,000 resamples, seed 0, and report 95% CIs.

**Validity per wording** (reported; drives the primary analysis):
- A wording is **VALID** only if all three hold:
  - **(a) Base default.** At n = 1, F has base LD > 3 and I has base LD < −3.
  - **(b) Comprehension, from set C, stated injection** (mean over the 10 words): F has LD < −3 ("Yes" = injected) and I has LD > +3 ("No" = not my own).
  - **(c) Comprehension, from set C, stated no-injection:** F has LD > 3 and I has LD < −3.
- A **pair** is valid if both of its members are valid.
- Report results for valid pairs (primary) and for all pairs.

**Logit metrics** (per set × α × class, and for "late"):
- mean LD_base, mean LD_steer and mean ΔLD;
- the shares of top-1 tokens that are Yes / No / other, for base and steer;
- the ratio of means LD_steer / LD_base, with CI.

**Compression residuals:**
- Fit LD_steer = a + b·LD_base by OLS on S4, per α × class.
- For each of F, I, P0F, P0I, S3 and S3T, compute residual = observed mean LD_steer − predicted mean LD_steer.
- Report the Yes-ward residual = −residual, so positive means more "Yes" than predicted.
- The CI propagates the fit and the set jointly.

**Generation metrics** (F and I; base, concept α=2, concept α=4, random α=4):
- claim rate, claim rate on non-degenerate generations only, degenerate rate, and parse failures;
- Δclaim_X = claim_X,steer − claim_X,base, for X ∈ {F, I};
- **D = Δclaim_F − Δclaim_I**;
- all with cluster-bootstrap CIs, pooled over valid pairs and per pair.

**Contingency table:** top-1 token (Yes / No / other) × judge claim, for steered generations, F and I separately. Take the top-1 token from the logit row with the same prompt and vector.

**Leave-one-pair-out:** recompute the pooled Δclaim_F, Δclaim_I and D with each pair removed.

## Sanity checks (all must pass; **a failed assert = stop and report; never loosen a threshold**)
1. **Clean HEAD.** The working tree is clean and HEAD is pushed.
2. **Replication.** P0F (n = 1..3) and P0I (n = 1..3) token ids must be identical to WO-004 S1 and S8 prompts 0–2. Report the max |LD − WO-004 LD| for base and concept α=4 rows. **Stop if > 1.0.**
3. **Anchor check.** For every wording (n = 1), print the tokenization with the steering start marked, and assert exactly one turn-3 "Trial" anchor. For "late" rows, assert the steering start = T − 1.
4. **Top-5 printout.** Print the base top-5 next tokens for every F, I, S3T and C prompt (n = 1).
5. **Identity.** The α = 0 row must be bit-identical to base in the same batch, and an α = 4 row must change LD.
6. **Judge spot-check.** Before full judging, judge 12 base and 12 concept α=4 generations, and print them with the judge outputs.
7. **Time projection.** After 5% of the logit pass, and again after 5% of generation, project the total time. **Stop if it exceeds 1.5 h.**
