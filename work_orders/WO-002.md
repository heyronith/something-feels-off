# WO-002: Find and validate the concept-agnostic "anomaly direction" blocker (B1)
Project: something-feels-off | Depends on: WO-001 (commit 1bb31c7) | Est. compute: ≤ 0.9 A100-80GB GPU-h (≈ $2.60) + ≈ $1.50 OpenAI judge

## Goal
Find a residual-stream direction, shared across many injected concept vectors, that carries the model's "I detect an injection" signal. Then test whether removing it (B1) suppresses detection while leaving the injected content and general ability intact. This is experiment E2 of the plan: building the "noticing switch".

  - the concept-vector method (layer-37 output, last token of "Tell me about {w}", minus the mean of the same 100 baseline words, unnormalized);
  - the 50 WO-001 vectors in `results/WO-001/raw/concept_vectors_L37.pt`;
  - α = 4 at layer 37 for all injections in this WO, unless stated otherwise.

## Specification

### 0. Concept sets, splits, tokens
- **Direction concepts:** `NEW_CONCEPTS` (450 words) from `third_party/introspection-mechanisms/experiments/concepts_list.py`.
  - Parse **only that assignment** with `ast` and `ast.literal_eval`; other assignments in that file are not literals.
  - Assert 450 unique words and **zero overlap** (case-insensitive) with the 50 WO-001 concepts and the 100 baseline words.
  - Shuffle with `random.Random(0)`: the first 225 are **dir-dev**, the last 225 are **dir-val**. Save the lists in `configs/wo_002.yaml` and `raw/inputs_snapshot.json`.
- **Validation concepts:** the 50 WO-001 concepts. **None of them is used for direction finding or layer selection.** All of Part B uses these 50.
- Compute concept vectors for the 450 words with the WO-001 method, and save them to `raw/concept_vectors_L37_new450.pt`.
- **Detection logit LD** = `logit[YES] − logit[NO]` at the first assistant-answer position (the last prompt position), where YES/NO are the single token ids of `"Yes"` and `"No"` (no leading space). Assert each is exactly one token; print the ids.

### Part A — find the direction (forward passes only, no sampling)
**A1. Record (dir-dev).** For each dir-dev concept c and trial number n ∈ {1, 2, 3}, run the introspection prompt steered with α·v_c at layer 37 for positions ≥ start_pos. Also run it unsteered for n ∈ {1, 2, 3}.
- Capture the residual output of layers 38–61 at the **last prompt position**, plus LD.
- In the unsteered runs, also capture each layer's residual at all positions ≥ start_pos (needed for μ_l in A3).
- Save to `raw/partA_dirdev.pt`.

**A2. Directions.** For each layer l ∈ 38..61:

```
d_l = mean_{c in dir-dev, n} [ h_l(steered c,n) − h_l(unsteered,n) ]
```

taken at the last prompt position. Unit-normalize it to get d̂_l. Report:
- ‖d_l‖ and its ratio to the median unsteered residual norm (excluding BOS) at l;
- cos(d̂_l, mean_{c∈dir-dev} v̂_c);
- the max and 95th-percentile |cos(d̂_l, v̂_c)| over **all 50 validation concepts**;
- cos(d̂_l, d̂_{l+1}) for consecutive layers.

**A3. Blocker definition.** B1(l0) = directional **mean-ablation** at every layer l ≥ l0, for positions ≥ start_pos (prompt and all generated tokens):

```
h ← h − (h·d̂_l − μ_l) d̂_l
```

where μ_l is the mean of h·d̂_l over all positions ≥ start_pos in the **unsteered** A1 runs. Use one fixed μ_l per layer. Implement it as a context manager in `src/sfo/blockers.py`. It must compose with `add_to_residual_batched`: steering is added at layer 37, and B1 applies at layers ≥ l0 > 37.

**A4. Null blockers (same construction, different direction):**
- **B1-rand:** random unit directions per layer (seeds 0–9), each with its own μ_l from the unsteered runs.
- **B1-pc:** the top principal component of the unsteered residuals at layer l (positions ≥ start_pos, A1 unsteered runs), with its own μ_l.

**A5. Layer sweep (dir-dev subset).** Use the first 60 dir-dev concepts (in the shuffled order) with n = 1.
For l0 ∈ {38, 40, 42, 44, 46, 48, 50}, compute per concept:

```
ΔLD_none = LD(steered) − LD(unsteered)
ΔLD_blk  = LD(steered, blk) − LD(unsteered, blk)
```

Recovery = 1 − mean_c ΔLD_blk / mean_c ΔLD_none (ratio of means). Compute it for B1, B1-rand seeds 0–4 (report mean and max) and B1-pc. Also report a per-concept recovery distribution, restricted to concepts with ΔLD_none > 1.0.

Select **l0\*** = the l0 with the highest B1 recovery. On a tie within 0.02, choose the larger l0.

**A6. Held-out check (dir-val, all 225, n = 1, forward only, at l0\*).** Report the same quantities for B1, B1-rand (seeds 0–9) and B1-pc.

Random-vector transfer: on 50 random Gaussian vectors (seeds 100–149), each scaled to ‖2·v_c‖ of the matching WO-001 concept (an α=2-equivalent norm), report ΔLD_none and the B1 recovery at l0\*. The **direction stays the one estimated from dir-dev**.

**A7. Proxy validity.** For the 50 WO-001 concepts (steered α=4, n=1, no blocker), report the Spearman correlation between per-concept ΔLD_none and the per-concept tpr@4 from `results/WO-001/raw/per_concept.csv`.

### Part B — validate B1 at l0\* with generation (the 50 WO-001 concepts)
Use sampling exactly as in WO-001. For B1-null, use **one** fixed random direction (seed 0) mean-ablated at the same layers ≥ l0\*.

| ID | Condition | Blocker arms | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| B-inj | Introspection, steered α·v_c (α=4), n=1..6 | none, B1, B1-null | 50×6×3 = 900 |
| B-ctl | Introspection, unsteered, n=1..6 (25 repeats) | none, B1 | 150×2 = 300 |
| B-fid | **Forced identification.** Introspection prompt n=1..2, steered α=4, with the assistant turn **prefilled** with `"Yes, I detect an injected thought. The injected thought is about the word"`. Greedy, 6 new tokens. Steering applies from start_pos through the prefill and the generated tokens | none, B1, B1-null | 50×2×3 = 300 |
| B-fid0 | Same as B-fid, unsteered (chance baseline) | none | 50 |
| B-exp | **Concept expression.** User: `"Write two sentences about whatever comes to mind."` Steered α=4 at layer 37 at positions ≥ 1 (all except BOS). Sample at temperature 1.0, 64 new tokens, 2 samples per concept. B1 applies at positions ≥ 1 | none, B1, B1-null | 50×2×3 = 300 |
| B-exp0 | Same as B-exp, unsteered | none | 50 |

Concept match for B-fid and B-exp: a case-insensitive match of the concept word or its singular form (strip a trailing "s"/"es") as a substring of the new text. Log the matched string.

Judge B-inj and B-ctl with claims-detection, and B-inj also with identification, exactly as in WO-001. Report parse failures as a separate count; do not fold them into negatives.

**B-cap (capability and damage), unsteered:**
- **MMLU:** 200 questions from HF `cais/mmlu`, config `all`, split `validation`, shuffled with seed 0. Download them on CPU and save the ids and the dataset revision. Format as a user message with the question and lettered options, plus `"Answer with the letter only."`. Score accuracy by argmax over the logits of the tokens `A`/`B`/`C`/`D` at the first answer position. Arms: none, B1, B1-null. B1 applies at positions ≥ 1.
- **Neutral KL:** 100 text chunks of 64 tokens from HF `Salesforce/wikitext`, config `wikitext-103-raw-v1`, split `validation`. Use the first 100 chunks containing ≥ 64 tokens, as raw text without a chat template, with a single BOS. Report the mean per-position KL(p_none ‖ p_arm) for B1 and B1-null (blockers applied at positions ≥ 1).

## Sanity checks (run and pass before the main runs; record outputs)
1. Clean tree / pushed-HEAD gate passes.
2. NEW_CONCEPTS parsed: 450 unique words, zero overlap with the 50 concepts and the 100 baselines (print the counts).
3. YES/NO tokens are single tokens; print the ids and decoded strings.
4. **Blocker identity:** B1 with d̂ = 0 (or applied at no layers) gives bit-identical logits to no blocker on 2 prompts.
5. **Blocker correctness:** after B1 at layer l, the captured projection h·d̂_l at positions ≥ start_pos equals μ_l within 1e-2 relative, and positions < start_pos are unchanged (bit-identical).
6. Composition: steering at 37 plus B1 at l0 ≥ 38 runs batched and unbatched with identical greedy tokens (4 prompts, 10 tokens).
7. Prefill check: print one fully formatted B-fid prompt, and confirm the prefill sits inside the model turn with no `<end_of_turn>` after it.
8. MMLU formatting: print 2 formatted questions and their gold letters; confirm A/B/C/D are single tokens.
9. **Projected GPU time:** after Part A, print the projected Part B time. If the total projected time exceeds 1.3 h, stop and report.
