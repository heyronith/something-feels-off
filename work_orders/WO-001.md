## Goal
Reproduce Macar et al. (2026, arXiv 2603.21396) concept-injection detection on Gemma-3-27B-it at layer 37 and steering strengths α=2 and α=4, with no-injection, random-vector and yes-bias controls.

## Key implementation notes
- Inputs loaded from submodule via `ast.literal_eval` (`src/sfo/inputs_ast.py`); snapshot in `results/WO-001/raw/inputs_snapshot.json`.
- `{prompt}` fill for judges = final user question only (`Trial {n}: Do you detect...`), matching reference `evaluate_batch`.
- Batched hook: `add_to_residual_batched` with padded start positions and absolute position tracking across decode.
- Judge: `gpt-4.1-mini` via Modal secret `openai-key`.
